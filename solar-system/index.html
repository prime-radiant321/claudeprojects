Solar System
(drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan)

Size comparison: every body is drawn at its true size relative to the others, lined up side by side. Distances are ignored here. The Sun is 109 Earths wide.

Distances are always to true scale (1 AU = 1 unit), with real periods and orbital inclinations. Bodies at true size are sub-pixel specks, so enlarge them with the slider (the Sun is capped at 20×, otherwise it would swallow the inner planets). The Moon's distance is true until the enlarged Earth would swallow it; then it is pushed out to 3 Earth radii. Planet textures by James Hastings-Trew (planetpixelemporium.com).
